I attended the *Being Human in the Age of AI:An AI Day Panel Discussion*. This event was very informative about how to approach AI. There were multiple questions asked for the panelist that consisted of their usage of AI and what they thought about what it means for the future of the human species. What stood out to me the most about this discussion was the different opinions that the panelists had about when they should use AI. One of the panelists said that he would use AI for everything, including writing love letters to his wife and making math study guides for his daughter. While another panelist disagreed about writing love letters using AI. This discussion provided a reflection on the morality of AI usage and how AI could be used for both good and bad things, it just comes down to the character of the person.
